# Trust and Verification in Estate Administration

## Overview

This project evaluates the reliability and trustworthiness of artificial intelligence when used to support estate administration and probate-related tasks.

The analysis focuses on the accuracy of AI-generated information, the importance of source verification, the risk of incomplete or misleading outputs, and the continuing need for human oversight in high-stakes decision-making environments.

## Problem Statement

Organizations are increasingly adopting AI tools to support administrative and decision-making functions. However, AI-generated outputs may omit critical context, misrepresent source material, or produce inaccurate recommendations.

This project examines whether AI can be relied upon in estate administration workflows and identifies governance controls necessary to ensure responsible use.

## Objectives

- Evaluate AI-generated responses in a real-world use case.
- Compare AI outputs against source materials.
- Assess the reliability of AI-generated recommendations.
- Identify risks associated with AI-assisted decision making.
- Determine where human oversight remains essential.

## Key Findings

- AI can accelerate information gathering and administrative tasks.
- AI-generated responses may omit important context.
- Source verification remains critical.
- Human review is necessary for consequential decisions.
- AI should be treated as a decision-support tool rather than a replacement for professional judgment.

## AI Governance Considerations

- Human-in-the-loop oversight
- Source validation and verification
- Transparency of AI-generated information
- Risk management for high-impact decisions
- Trustworthy AI practices
- Accountability for outcomes
