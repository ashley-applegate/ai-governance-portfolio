# Gender Bias Risk Assessment and Governance Framework for AI in Early Education

## Overview

This project evaluates gender bias risks associated with artificial intelligence systems used in early education environments.

The analysis examines how bias in AI-enabled educational technologies may reinforce gender stereotypes, limit educational opportunities, and contribute to unequal outcomes for students.

The project was developed as a policy brief for educational policymakers and includes governance recommendations designed to improve fairness, accountability, transparency, and oversight.

## Key Governance Issues

- Gender bias in AI training data
- Unequal educational opportunities
- Lack of transparency in AI systems
- Inconsistent implementation across schools
- Limited oversight and auditing requirements

## Stakeholders

- Students
- Parents and guardians
- Teachers
- School administrators
- Educational technology developers
- U.S. Department of Education

## Governance Recommendations

- Independent bias testing
- Regular bias audits
- Transparency requirements for developers
- Human oversight of AI systems
- Ongoing monitoring and research
