# AI-Supported Postsecondary Advising Program Evaluation

## Overview

This project evaluates the design and implementation of an AI-supported advising program intended to improve postsecondary outcomes for rural students.

The analysis examines how artificial intelligence can be integrated into advising processes while maintaining appropriate governance, privacy protections, human oversight, and accountability.

## Problem Statement

Students in rural communities often face barriers to postsecondary education, including limited access to advising resources and individualized guidance.

AI-assisted advising systems have the potential to increase access and support decision-making, but they also introduce risks related to privacy, bias, accuracy, and oversight.

## Key Governance Considerations

- Human oversight of AI recommendations
- Data privacy and student protections
- Bias and fairness concerns
- Accuracy of AI-generated guidance
- Transparency and accountability
- Program monitoring and evaluation
